Help find moving objects in NASA SPHEREx images

NASA’s SPHEREx telescope maps the whole sky in infrared. Each task shows the same small patch of sky a few hours apart. Asteroids drift between the pictures; stars stay put. A few seconds per answer is all it takes.

How to answer

  1. Watch the dot inside the ring while the pictures blink (or step through them with the arrows).
  2. If the dot jumps to a new spot between pictures, choose Moving.
  3. If it stays in the same place, choose Not moving, even if it gets brighter or fainter.

Some tasks are known asteroids or known stars. They let us weigh everyone’s answers fairly, so take your time and go with what you see. Every answer is combined with other people’s into a consensus about each candidate.

Sky Task is a NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 project, not a NASA website. Images: NASA SPHEREx via NASA/IPAC IRSA. Known asteroids: NASA/JPL Small-Body Identification API. We store your answers and their timing with a random session id; no account, name or email.
